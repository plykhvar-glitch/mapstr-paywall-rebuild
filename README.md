# Mapstr — mapstr plus, fed by the two answers before it

**Live page: https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/mapstr-paywall-rebuild/**

The last two onboarding steps of Mapstr ("Why do you want to use mapstr?" and "Must-have maps"),
the mapstr plus paywall and a new offer on close, rebuilt in Mapstr's own design. Change the answers
on the two onboarding screens and the paywall rewrites itself. Flip **Today** to see the version that
ships now. English, French and Italian, light and dark, plus a 30-second walkthrough.

## The five changes

| # | Change | Today | Now | Expected impact |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | A second chance on close | The X goes straight to "It's better with your friends!" | A sheet comparing free and mapstr plus (300 places vs unlimited, Overlay maps, AI, alerts, tables) and the monthly plan | ARPU +10–15% |
| 2 | The maps she just followed, on her map | A generic map illustration, "Unlock all features right now" | "Julia, the 3 maps you follow, on your own map", with their pins and a working Overlay maps switch | CR +15–20% |
| 3 | The first slide answers why she came | Same feature order for everyone | Plan a trip → proximity alerts and tables; save places → unlimited and bulk editing; and so on | CR +10–15% |
| 4 | Your proof next to the price | One review card, below the timeline | 4.7 ★ from 30K ratings, 4M+ users, 105M+ places saved, right under the plans | CR +10–15% |
| 5 | The annual saving, said | "The more popular" only | "Save 38%" on the annual plan | ARPU +10–15% |

*Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps —
not measured lift for Mapstr.*

Prices are the European examples on Mapstr's FAQ (59 €/year, 7,90 €/month); the 38% saving is
computed from them. The rating comes from the French App Store listing; user and place counts from
mapstr.com; features and copy from Mapstr's own screens and App Store screenshots.
